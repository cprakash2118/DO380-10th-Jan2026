#- Pod Scheduling Concepts:-
Point-01-# What is POD Scheduling ? [w.r.t OpenSHift] 
  - POD Scheduling - Process by which OpenSHift decides on which node a POD should run/placed.
  Key Idea:-
    - When you deploy application (via POD, Deployment, StatefulSet etc), you do NOT choose the node.
	- OpenSHift "built-in" scheduler makes that decision.
	
  - Why scheduling is needed?
    - Cluster have multiple nodes.
	- Nodes differ in:-
	  - CPU & Memory
	  - Hardwre (GPU, Fast CPU, SSD, Standard CPU)
	  - Network locality 
	  - Availability zones 
	- Scheduler ensures:-
	  - Best performance 
	  - High Availability
	  - Efficient resource usages.
